Privacy

This website may collect a small amount of information when visitors choose to leave feedback.

What is collected?

If you submit feedback, the website may ask for your name and email address, along with the feedback you choose to provide.

Why is it collected?

Your name and email address are collected only to identify the person submitting the feedback and, when necessary, to respond to the feedback.

How is the information used?

The information provided through the feedback form is used only for managing and responding to feedback about this website.

Is the information shared?

Personal information submitted through the feedback form is not intended to be publicly displayed or sold to third parties.

Your choice

Providing your name and email address is voluntary. Visitors can choose whether or not to submit feedback.
